## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intersection of high-fashion editorial rigor and underground luxury streetwear. It projects audacious confidence, kinetic energy, and curatorial precision. The visual tone is unapologetically expressive, built around vivid chromatic collisions grounded by stark architectural discipline.

The visual strategy fuses **High-Contrast Editorial** with modern **Digital Brutalism**:
- **Monolithic Scale:** Razor-sharp typography pairings where sweeping, high-contrast serif displays collide with austere, technical grotesques.
- **Color-Blocking as Structure:** Saturated pigment planes establish spatial zones rather than serving as mere accents.
- **Architectural Edge:** Structural hairline dividers, modular editorial grids, and tactile micro-states that mimic high-end editorial layouts and runway programs.
- **Tactile Polish:** Ultra-sleek pill badges, bespoke product taxonomy tags, and micro-interactions calibrated for punchy feedback.

## Colors

The palette leverages pure, saturated tones engineered for visceral impact against high-fashion alabaster and deep obsidian backdrops.

- **Primary (`#2563EB` - Electric Cobalt):** Dominant brand anchor; applied to key focal moments, primary navigation callouts, dynamic color-blocked hero planes, and active state indicators.
- **Secondary (`#F97316` - Vivid Tangerine):** High-tension warmth; utilized for urgent e-commerce triggers, limited edition badges, drop countdowns, and secondary focal elements.
- **Tertiary (`#84CC16` - Acid Lime):** Electric counterpoint; reserved for promotional tags, avant-garde callouts, micro-indicators, and stark contrast over obsidian surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#09090B` - Deep Obsidian):** Used for razor-sharp typography, structural grid rules, and high-contrast editorial backdrops.
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC` - Alabaster Cream):** Provides a clean, tactile backdrop that prevents chromatic fatigue and lets collection photography command attention.

Apply color blocking deliberately: allow adjacent panels of solid Electric Cobalt and Acid Lime to frame stark monochrome lookbook imagery.

## Typography

Typography establishes tension between classical couture drama and underground design.

- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** High-contrast vertical stems and hairline serifs inject high-fashion editorial authority. Used for hero collection releases, statement campaign titles, and drop numbers.
- **Interface & Body (`Syne`):** Modern grotesque sans with distinctive character geometries. Delivers sharp legibility across navigational items, micro-specs, lookbook metadata, and e-commerce transactions.
- **Case Conventions:** Apply full uppercase treatment to `label-lg`, `label-sm`, and `title-md` for technical garment taxonomy, sizing matrices, and navigational breadcrumbs. Maintain sentence case or title case in `display-hero` to preserve high-contrast ligature elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a rigid, modular editorial architecture that shifts seamlessly between asymmetric editorial spreads and disciplined commerce listings:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Generous `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Enables asymmetric fashion spreads where a 7-column hero shot offsets a 5-column technical product breakdown.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standardized `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Two-to-four column product grid conversions.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Compact `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`. Clean full-bleed presentation for garments, collapsing split-pane lookbooks into stackable horizontal carousels and edge-to-edge modular cards.

Spatial discipline is preserved by snapping all card padding, section gaps, and swatch clusters directly to the strict `space-*` scale.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy, diffused SaaS drop shadows in favor of **Crisp Architectural Outlines** and **High-Tension Planar Layering**:

- **Hairline Outlines:** Pure 1px structural borders using Obsidian (`#09090B`) or tinted Cobalt (`#2563EB`) create defined zones across light canvases.
- **Hard Offset Edge (Drop State):** Critical floating elements (such as cart drawers, promotional popouts, or modal lookbooks) employ crisp 0-blur hard shadows: `4px 4px 0px #09090B`.
- **Z-Index Layering:** Visual hierarchy is built through solid, overlapping modular panels and stark color-blocked backdrops rather than ambient blurred shadows.
- **Image Frames:** Editorial photography is uncontained by soft drop shadows; it relies entirely on razor-sharp bounding rules and edge-to-edge color-blocked margins.

## Shapes

The architectural foundation relies on sharp **0px (`roundedness: 0`)** geometries across content panels, cards, interactive inputs, and layout frames. This unrounded structure preserves the raw, brutalist editorial precision of high-fashion print layouts.

**Intentional Contrast Exception:** 
To balance the dominant sharp edges, specific micro-elements use full-radius pill styling:
- Product status tags (`NEW DROP`, `SOLD OUT`)
- Interactive color swatches and sizing chips
- Dynamic floating pill buttons for rapid shopping actions

This deliberate clash between sharp angular containers and sleek pill controls produces the signature avant-garde tension.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full Obsidian (`#09090B`) or Electric Cobalt (`#2563EB`) background, zero border radius, stark white `label-lg` uppercase typography. Active/hover state inverts instantly or shifts +2px down and right with an Acid Lime border swap.
- **Secondary Editorial:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid Obsidian or Electric Cobalt border, instant solid fill on hover.
- **Floating Quick-Add (Pill Exception):** Compact rounded pill (`rounded-full`), padded `0.5rem 1.25rem`, utilizing pure white canvas with hard 1px outline for hovering over garment imagery.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Category Chips:** Sharp rectangular containers (`roundedness: 0`) with 1px border. Selected state fills with solid Electric Cobalt with crisp white text.
- **Collection Badges:** Pill-shaped tags (`border-radius: 9999px`) in Acid Lime (`#84CC16`) with black text or Tangerine (`#F97316`) with white text, positioned at the upper corner of product imagery.

### Product Lookbook Cards
- Edge-to-edge image containers resting on Alabaster bases, framed with thin 1px Obsidian bottom and side borders.
- Image aspect ratio locked strictly to 3:4 or 4:5 fashion proportions.
- Hover interaction triggers an instant secondary garment angle swap without blurred cross-fades, maintaining editorial immediacy.

### Color Swatch Selectors
- Circular disc swatches (20px diameter) bounded by a 1px offset ring when active.
- Support multi-tone swatch split-discs to indicate dual-tone color-blocked streetwear garments (e.g., half Electric Cobalt, half Acid Lime).

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Underlined or sharp boxed inputs (`roundedness: 0`) featuring 1px solid Obsidian borders.
- Floating `label-sm` positioned in high-tracking uppercase.
- Focus state thickens border to 2px Electric Cobalt without glow rings.

### Lists & Lookbook Indices
- Run-of-show numbered indices with 1px hairline horizontal separators.
- Item hover highlights the entire row in soft Off-White with an instantaneous preview thumbnail reveal following cursor movement.
